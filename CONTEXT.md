# PolicyCraft vocabulary

- **Client organization**: a company whose policies are created in PolicyCraft. Each policy belongs to one client organization.
- **ESG organization**: a client organization registered in the shared ESG platform. Its company details remain managed in that platform.
- **PolicyCraft organization**: a client organization created by a PolicyCraft admin for internal policy work. Only admins and its assigned managers can access it; it has no ESG organization registration.
- **Organization profile**: the saved company details used to initialize new policies. Editing the profile does not change the company details already saved in a policy.
- **Client user**: an existing client account that creates and edits policies within its organization.
- **Manager**: an internal staff member who works on policies for assigned client organizations. Several managers may share an organization.
- **Admin**: an internal staff member who manages managers and assignments and works on policies across client organizations.
- **Assignment**: permission for a manager to work in a client organization's shared policy workspace.
- **Invitation**: an expiring request for a named recipient to accept manager access.
- **Policy creator**: the account that originally created a policy; reassignment and later edits do not change this attribution.
- **Policy task**: an administrator's request for a manager to create a policy for an assigned client organization by a deadline. This is distinct from an organization assignment, which grants access.
- **Manager progress**: the filled sections in a manager's latest saved policy. Later client-user or administrator edits do not change that manager's recorded progress.
- **Task completion**: the manager's explicit declaration that an assigned task is finished; it is separate from the filled-section percentage.
