# PolicyCraft vocabulary

- **Client organization**: a company whose policies are created in PolicyCraft. Each policy belongs to one client organization.
- **Client user**: an existing client account that creates and edits policies within its organization.
- **Manager**: an internal staff member who works on policies for assigned client organizations. Several managers may share an organization.
- **Admin**: an internal staff member who manages managers and assignments and works on policies across client organizations.
- **Assignment**: permission for a manager to work in a client organization's shared policy workspace.
- **Invitation**: an expiring request for a named recipient to accept manager access.
- **Policy creator**: the account that originally created a policy; reassignment and later edits do not change this attribution.
